# Lamp room rota

The rota decides who climbs the tower on which night and who cleans the lens in the morning.

The order was rewritten after the [[foghorn-repair|horn work]] ran two weekends long, and the club recorded the reason in [[2026-01-04_workshop-notes#^compressor|the workshop notes]].
